At first glance the individual looked plain, but the details proved unexpectedly ornate under magnification.
The head was mostly ivory with a smoky patch surrounding each eye, and the frontal area carried a shallow groove that widened toward the clypeal margin.
Across the mesosoma, the cuticle shifted from bronze to olive depending on light angle, while the pronotal corners formed low triangular lobes rather than true spines.
The legs contrasted strongly with the body: the mid and hind femora were dark mahogany, yet the tarsi were pale and almost translucent.
No expanded setal fans were present on the tibiae; only scattered erect hairs occurred along the outer edges.
The terminal abdominal segments were narrowly banded in cream, and the apical sternite ended in a rounded notch flanked by two short, inward-curving setae.
