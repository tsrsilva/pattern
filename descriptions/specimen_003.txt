Clypeus amber.
Thorax matte black.
Abdomen chestnut with pale terminal band.
Forelegs yellow-brown; hind legs darkened.
Clypeus with a narrow median ridge.
Thorax with paired blunt spines on the posterior margin.
Abdomen with sparse short setae, denser near the apex.
Fore tibia with a single straight spur; claws small and sharply pointed.
