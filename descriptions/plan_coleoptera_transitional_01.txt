Head flat, eyes relatively large, moderately convex; temples present, short; occiput without raised areas; antennal scape elongate, widened apically, subequal in length to antennomeres 2-4; antennomere 2 short, moniliform; antennomeres 3-10 relatively elongate, increasingly wide and triangular to antennomere 10, very slightly asymmetrical; antennomere 11 elongate, narrowed near midlength; lateral mandibular tooth indistinct. 
Pronotum narrow, elongate; anterior margin moderately emarginate; anterolateral angles rounded; lateral margins of disc straight to slightly sinuate, divergent slightly anteriorly; hind angles obtuse, rounded; posterior margin with narrow lobe; anterior setose patch present, moderately raised; pronotal pits marked by shallow grooves; procoxal process long, narrow, visible part extended posterior of coxae and expanded, making contact with mesosternum; hypomeral edges narrowly separated. 
Elytra elongate, parallel-sided; disc flat, lateral carinae indistinctly to slightly developed; apex abbreviated, posterior margin slightly sinuate, exposing most of last visible tergite; decumbent vestiture short, but visible; apical patch elongate, narrow. 
Color dark rufous; dorsum of elytra slightly lighter in color than forebody; lateral areas of elytra infuscated.
