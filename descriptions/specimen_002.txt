The creature possessed the most amazing features one entomologist had ever the joy of observing.
The head had these beautiful, although disturbingly-shaped, curved and crooked markings that resembled one of the uncanny engravings seen at valleys once inhabited by old civilizations.
Incredibly, the whole body of this individual had a greenish, iridescent hue that resembled deeply that of the carnivorous flies inhabiting the old forests of Northern South America.
I was bewildered to see that the creature's knees and toes were not as splendid as the whole of its visage: alas, the creature had pale legs.
